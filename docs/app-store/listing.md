# Fathom — App Store listing (draft)

Everything here follows the product's claims rule: Fathom **trains arousal
regulation and shows you a measurement**. It never says it treats, reduces or
cures anxiety or anything else. Apple reviews health-adjacent wording closely;
this wording is the same the app itself uses.

## Description (4000)

Fathom is a dive you take with your breath.

Two breaths in, then one long, slow breath out — and your diver sinks. The
longer and slower you breathe out, the deeper you go: through the twilight
zone, into the midnight zone, down to the abyss. Your dive light charges as you
breathe in and shows you what lives in the dark.

That pattern — a double breath in and an extended breath out — is called
cyclic sighing. Fathom guides you into it, slows the rhythm gently as you go,
and measures how fast you were breathing at the start and at the end, so you
can see what changed.

WHAT YOU GET
• A five-minute dive, or a ninety-second quick dive for right before an exam,
  a race or a hard conversation
• Your breathing rate before and after, shown plainly — including when nothing
  changed
• A field journal of eighteen creatures and places to find, from lanternfish to
  hydrothermal vents, each with one true fact
• An underwater soundscape that muffles as you sink, and haptics you can feel
  with your eyes closed
• Play with the microphone, or by holding a finger on the screen

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your breath is measured on your iPhone. Nothing is recorded, uploaded or
  stored on a server.
• No account, no ads, no tracking, no streaks, no notifications.

WHAT IT IS NOT
Fathom is not therapy, not a diagnosis and not for a crisis. It trains arousal
regulation and shows you a measurement — that is the whole claim. If you are in
crisis, please talk to a person: 988 (US), 9-8-8 (Canada), or
findahelpline.com.

Take care: not while driving or doing anything that needs your attention. If
slow breathing makes you lightheaded, stop and breathe normally. If you have a
heart or lung condition, are pregnant, or have a history of fainting or
seizures, check with a clinician before making a habit of it.

## Review notes (for App Review)

- The microphone is used only to measure the sound of the user's breath out,
  on device, ~50 times a second (a few numbers per frame, no recording). The
  usage string says so.
- Every dive can be played without the microphone: Settings → Breathing mode →
  Touch and hold.
- No account, no network calls.
- The app makes no medical claims. It shows breathing rate before and after a
  session and says plainly that it is not therapy or diagnosis, with crisis
  resources on the first-run safety card and on every results screen.
