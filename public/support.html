Fathom for iPhone

The microphone doesn’t hear my breath

Breathe out through your mouth, slowly, with the iPhone at chest height about an arm’s length away or closer. A quiet room helps; music or a fan nearby can hide a soft breath. If it still doesn’t work, use Settings → Breathing mode → Touch and hold, and hold a finger on the screen for as long as you breathe out.

I declined microphone access

Open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and turn Fathom on, then choose the microphone again in Fathom’s settings.

My dive ended by itself

A dive ends if you leave the app, take a call, or the microphone is taken away (by Siri, an alarm, or plugging in headphones). Fathom stops rather than guess at breaths it could not hear.

Apple Health

Turn on Apple Health in Fathom’s settings to save each dive as mindful minutes. If you declined access, allow it in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Fathom.

Deleting my data

Everything Fathom saves stays on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it. Minutes already written to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app.

What Fathom is not

Fathom is not therapy, not a diagnosis and not for a crisis. If you are in crisis, please talk to a person: 988 (US), 9-8-8 (Canada), or findahelpline.com.
